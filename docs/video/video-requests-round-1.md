# Cruise Adventure - Video Requests, Round 1

Short clips that play inside the game. The kids talk to the player, who is a young child (Becca is 4 and can't read), so every instruction is spoken.

**Already done and accepted:** `Videos/becca-pack.mp4` (Becca's intro). Its style and length are the model for everything below. Ryan's bar is "recognizable", so small differences in furniture or styling are fine.

**Rules for every clip:**
- **Attach as references:** `Character Sheets/Jack-Jack and Becca Character Sheets v0.4.png` and the room background named in the clip.
- **Characters:** Jack-Jack (6) has golden sandy dirty-blonde hair (no copper or orange), an olive green hoodie and blue jeans. Becca (4) has curly blonde hair, brown eyes, a pink hoodie and navy leggings. Hands empty, no props unless stated.
- **Format:** 16:9, 720p or 1080p, **8-10 seconds**, a locked-off camera at child eye level, no camera moves.
- **No text, captions, logos or music.** The game adds its own sound.
- **Start and end on a held still frame** (half a second each), with both kids standing still and smiling. The game cuts in and out of the clip there.
- **Voices:** a bright, clear, slow-enough-for-a-toddler kid voice: Becca a sweet 4-year-old girl, Jack-Jack an energetic 6-year-old boy.
- **Save to** `Videos/` with the name shown.

## Priority 1 - `jack-pack.mp4` (Jack-Jack's intro; the game uses it in v0.2)
**Room:** `Game Assets/Backgrounds/Jack Room - Empty Background v0.1 - PROVISIONAL.png` (blue walls, star curtains, blue rug, monster truck poster).
