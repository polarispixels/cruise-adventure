This is every line that is not yet recorded: the airport's 15 lines (Voice Lines v0.5, never recorded; Becca records all 15 and Jack-Jack 12, because three of them are only heard in Jack-Jack's game) plus all the new lines for chapters 9 to 14. As always, the helper sibling says the instructions, so **Jack-Jack records the lines heard in Becca's game and Becca records the lines heard in Jack-Jack's game.** Each of you also records the lines that play in both games.

- **Becca records 89 lines** (for Jack-Jack's game).
- **Jack-Jack records 85 lines** (for Becca's game).

## How to record

- One file per line, named by the line id, saved in a folder for the kid who is speaking: `Voice/Becca/<line-id>.m4a` or `Voice/Jack-Jack/<line-id>.m4a` (for example `Voice/Becca/find-seat.m4a`). Any phone voice memo app is fine, and `.wav` works too.
- Record in a quiet room, about a hand's length from the phone, with half a second of silence before and after.
- The lines are short. Say them bright and slow, like talking to a little one. Saying it differently than written is fine; natural beats exact.
- Lines that end in "!" are happy. "Good night" style lines can be a whisper.
- A line heard in only one game says "Jack-Jack only" or "Becca only" in the "When it plays" column, meaning that kid's game. If the same words are in both lists, they are recorded by each kid.
- Cheers like "You found it!", "Yay! Good job!" and "Hmm... try another one!" are already recorded and are reused. Nothing about them needs to be redone.
