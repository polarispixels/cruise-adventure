# Cruise Adventure - Voice Lines v0.3

Every line is said by the **helper** kid, the sibling standing beside the player, speaking to the player. Each kid records every line, because each is the helper when the other is played.

**Recording:** one file per line, named by the ID (e.g. `find-pajamas.m4a` or `.wav`), any phone voice memo app is fine. Record in a quiet room, about a hand's length from the phone, with half a second of silence before and after. Lines are short, so say them bright and slow, like talking to a little one. Saying it differently than written is fine; natural beats exact.

Put the files in two folders, `Voice/Jack-Jack/` and `Voice/Becca/`, in the Kids Packing Video project, and the game picks them up.
