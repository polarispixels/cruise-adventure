# Cruise Adventure - Art Requests Round 7: The Flight and Hello Florida

> **Status (Nov 1, 2026):** open. The names, canvases and counts come from the builders' art lists on the chapter 9 and chapter 10 branches. Where a branch differs from the chapter spec, the branch wins and the difference is noted.

Two chapters need art. **Chapter 9, The flight:** the kids find their seats on an airplane, put the family's bags away, buckle up, pick a snack and spot cloud shapes out the window. **Chapter 10, Hello Florida:** the bags come around a carousel, then the child puts two booster seats and the family's eight bags into a rental minivan, and the family sleeps at a hotel by the ship. Full designs are in `Specs/Cruise Adventure - Chapter 09 - The Flight v0.1.md` and `Specs/Cruise Adventure - Chapter 10 - Hello Florida v0.1.md`.

**The same rules as before:** the same cartoon style, outlines and colors as the characters, props and car art you have already made. Character Sheets v0.4 still win (Becca's eyes are brown; Jack-Jack's hair is golden sandy dirty-blonde, no copper or orange). **No words, letters, numbers, brands or logos anywhere.** The game draws any digits. Generic, bright, friendly places, not copies of any real one. File names must match exactly: **placeholders are what the game shows today, and real art replaces them by file name with no code changes.** Save to the folder named under each section.

**Display-size rule (T3), for every file below.** Canvases are the size the game shows the picture, no more than about 1.25x its on-screen size on a 1280 x 800 tablet. Please generate at exactly the canvas listed and do not make anything bigger; backgrounds are 1672 x 941. Smaller files keep the game light.

**The airplane is generic:** a bright cabin, no airline name, tail color, logo or marks. **The rental is a plain family minivan:** no make, model name or badge anywhere. In Florida the family drives this minivan with two booster seats (blue for Jack-Jack, pink for Becca); at home they drive the black SUV from Round 5.

**Where the Chapter 10 branch differs from its spec (the branch wins):**

- Smaller canvases at display size: both boosters are 250 x 250 (spec 300 x 300), six of the decoys are 210 x 210 (spec 250 x 250), and `decoy-yellow-bag.png` is 420 x 170 (spec 400 x 160).
- `hotel-room-night.png` and `luggage-cart.png` are in the spec but not used by the build, so they are **not requested** this round. The hotel night is a clip or a still (see Video Requests Round 4).
- The minivan's two seat spots (the game drops the boosters at about x 410 to 710 and x 960 to 1260, y 450 to 750 on the interior) and its cargo area (about x 400 to 1600, y 250 to 1450 on the 2000 x 1600 open view) must be left bare.
- Becca's decoys are simple shapes in solid colors, very different from the family's bags. Jack-Jack's decoys are look-alikes (a near-blue case with a white stripe, a near-pink case with a sticker, and a yellow bag with a different strap).
