# Cruise Adventure - Art Requests Round 6: The Airport

> **Status (Oct 31, 2026):** delivered and in the game. All 22 airport files and the four poses, plus the maze wall color (`#7E9CAF` / `#263444`, 12 px corners) and the token face circle from the handoff.

The next chapter after the car is **The airport**. The family arrives, and the kids get everyone to the airplane in three short games:

1. **Goodbye suitcases.** At the check-in counter, the child puts the matching colored tag on each big suitcase and drags it onto the belt. The bags ride away through rubber flaps.
2. **Through security.** The child puts their backpack, tablet and buddy into gray trays, pushes the trays through the scanner, walks through the arch, and collects everything on the other side.
3. **Find our gate.** Seen from above, the child drags their own face through a maze of terminal hallways to the gate with the right airplane.

Please make everything in this list in one run. Same cartoon style, outlines and colors as the characters, props and car art you've already made. Character Sheets v0.4 still win (Becca's eyes are brown; Jack-Jack's hair is golden sandy dirty-blonde, no copper or orange). The family SUV is **black**, as in Round 5. A generic, bright, friendly airport, not a copy of any real one. **No words, letters or numbers anywhere**, including signs, screens and tags. Ryan's bar is "recognizable". Stop for review at the end.

The full chapter design is in `Specs/Cruise Adventure - Chapter 08 - The Airport v0.1.md` if you want the details.
