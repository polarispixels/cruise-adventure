# Cruise Adventure - Art Requests Round 8: Welcome Aboard and Sea Day

> **Status (Nov 1, 2026):** open. The names, canvases and counts come from the builders' art lists on the chapter 11 and chapter 13 branches. Where a branch differs from the chapter spec, the branch wins and the difference is noted.

Two chapters on the ship need art. **Chapter 11, Welcome aboard:** the kids get a key card at the welcome desk, find their room in a hallway of doors, and practice the safety drill so the ship's horn is not scary. **Chapter 13, Sea day:** the kids put on swimsuits, visit the big water slide (Jack-Jack rides, Becca is a little too small and splashes instead), play in the splash zone, and dress for dinner. Full designs are in `Specs/Cruise Adventure - Chapter 11 - Welcome Aboard v0.1.md` and `Specs/Cruise Adventure - Chapter 13 - Sea Day v0.1.md`.

**The same rules as before:** the same cartoon style, outlines and colors as the characters, props and car art you have already made. Character Sheets v0.4 still win (Becca's eyes are brown; Jack-Jack's hair is golden sandy dirty-blonde, no copper or orange). **No words, letters, numbers, brands or logos anywhere.** The game draws any digits. Generic, bright, friendly places, not copies of any real one. File names must match exactly: **placeholders are what the game shows today, and real art replaces them by file name with no code changes.** Save to the folder named under each section.

**Display-size rule (T3), for every file below.** Canvases are the size the game shows the picture, no more than about 1.25x its on-screen size on a 1280 x 800 tablet. Please generate at exactly the canvas listed and do not make anything bigger; backgrounds are 1672 x 941. Smaller files keep the game light.

**The ship is generic, "our big ship":** white, with a top-deck pool and a twisting open water slide seen from outside. No line name, logo, funnel colors or lettering anywhere. The inside is bright and friendly: soft carpet, round portholes, warm lights.
