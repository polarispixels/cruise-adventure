Please make everything in this list in one run. Same cartoon style, outlines and colors as the characters and props you've already made. Character Sheets v0.4 still win (Becca's eyes are brown; Jack-Jack's hair is golden sandy dirty-blonde, no copper or orange). No words or letters anywhere.

## How the puzzle works (why the sizes matter)

The trunk is an invisible grid of squares, **7 squares wide and 5 squares tall**. The two bottom corner squares are taken up by the **wheel-well bumps**, so they can't hold a bag. Every bag covers an exact block of squares, and the game snaps it into place. So each bag must be drawn to fill its own rectangle exactly. If a bag's picture is the wrong shape, the puzzle looks wrong.

In the art, **one square = 200 pixels**.

```
 . . . . . . .      7 x 5 squares, seen through the open hatch
 . . . . . . .      # = wheel-well bump (no bag fits there)
 . . . . . . .
 . . . . . . .
 # . . . . . #
```
