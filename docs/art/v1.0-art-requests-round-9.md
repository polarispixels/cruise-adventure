**Notes for Chapter 12:**

- **Day 1 and day 2 variants.** Day 1 is a bright beach with a striped towel at far left. Day 2 is a different cove: a rocky outcrop at left, other palm trees, a small wooden pier at far right and a few clouds. Both beaches keep the **same flat sand patch** at center and right, so the castle slots sit in the same place.
- **Six shells each day.** The branch has `shell-moon.png` (day 1) and `shell-limpet-2.png` (day 2) as the sixth shell of each day, so that Jack-Jack has enough different decoys. Each shell is a different shape and color so a child can match one to the card. The targets are the pink spiral (day 1) and the orange scallop (day 2).
- **Castle pieces** are sand-colored and molded as if made with a bucket. Pieces 2 and 3 (the side towers) are pre-built for Becca, so the same files serve both kids. Piece 1 is the gate (day 2: the arch), 4 is the keep (day 2: the left round tower), 5 is the top (day 2: the right round tower).
- The top-down sand files (`island-sand.png`, `island-sand-2.png`) are plain: the game scatters the shells on them.
- The two stills `island-arrival.png` and `island-splash.png` are requested with the clips in Video Requests Round 4.
