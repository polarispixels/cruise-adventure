# Cruise Adventure v0.2 - Game Art Requests

You are making art for **Cruise Adventure**, a Godot tablet game for Jack-Jack (6) and Becca (4). It's built on the packing video's characters and rooms. The game developer, a separate agent working in another repo, will pull your finished PNGs into the game. Please follow the technical specs exactly: the game swaps and lines these files up automatically, so a wrong size, a painted background or a shifted pose breaks the game.

Work in this order and stop for Ryan's review after each request: **Request 1 (backgrounds) -> Request 2 (characters) -> Request 3 (props)**. Put everything under `Game Assets/` in this project. Keep your usual `vX.Y - PROVISIONAL` naming, prompt notes and verification records. The developer copies files out and renames them on its side.

Character continuity is unchanged: use the approved sheets (`Character Sheets/Jack-Jack and Becca Character Sheets v0.4.png`, `jack-jack-character-reference v0.3.png`, `becca-character-reference v0.2.png`). Jack-Jack has sandy light-brown (dirty blonde) hair, an olive green hoodie, blue jeans, and gray/yellow sneakers. Becca has curly blonde hair, BROWN eyes, a pink hoodie, navy leggings, and pink/white sneakers. Same warm, polished hand-drawn cartoon style, confident dark outlines, soft shading. No text, logos or brand marks anywhere.

`Game Assets/Backgrounds/Jack Room - Empty Background v0.1` works for the game. No regeneration is needed. If it's ever regenerated, follow the specs below.

### 1b. Becca's room (new)
Becca's own empty bedroom, the counterpart to Jack-Jack's room.

**The layout must match Jack-Jack's room** so the game can use the same positions in both. Use the same camera height and angle, the same pulled-back wide view at child eye level, the floor line at the same height, and the same furniture positions:
- a dresser on the left (same size and position as Jack-Jack's, all drawers closed)
- a window centered toward the back, with curtains
- a single bed along the right, with a bedside table
- a large oval rug in the center, with the center and foreground floor fully clear

Make it **Becca's**: a pink and white palette with soft lavender accents, and a pink rug. Her curtains could have hearts or flowers instead of stars. Wall art: keep a framed cruise-ship picture above the dresser (it ties both rooms to the trip) and replace the monster truck with a **white unicorn with a rainbow mane** picture. Put a small picture over the bed. Cheerful warm daylight, as in Jack-Jack's room.

The room is empty: no people, characters, suitcases, checklist cards, loose clothing, toys on the floor, ears headband or plush unicorn. Those are packing items and the game adds them.

## Request 2 - Characters (the most important)

The game shows each child as a **full-body cut-out on a transparent background**. Animation works by **swapping between poses** (and the game adds bounces, squash and tilt in code), so every pose of one child must be lined up identically on the same canvas, like frames of a flip-book.

### Poses needed - for EACH child (Jack-Jack and Becca)
| # | Pose name | Description | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `idle` | Standing relaxed, weight even, arms at sides, friendly open smile, eyes toward the viewer | Default, title screen, waiting |
| 2 | `wave-1` | Same as idle, but the right hand (the child's own right) raised beside the head, open palm toward the viewer, big smile | Waving |
| 3 | `wave-2` | **Identical to wave-1 except the raised hand is tilted the other way** (about 30 degrees), so flipping 1 -> 2 -> 1 reads as a wave | Waving |
| 4 | `cheer` | Both arms straight up, open hands, mouth wide open in delight, eyes happy and squinting. Feet stay on the ground: the game adds the jump | Celebrating a correct item |
| 5 | `clap` | Hands together in front of the chest mid-clap, delighted face | The helper sibling reacting |
| 6 | `point` | One arm extended out to the side at shoulder height, pointing toward the viewer's right, encouraging smile, looking where they point | Hinting "it goes there!" |

That's 12 images in total (6 poses x 2 children).

**How to keep them consistent:** make `idle` first and get it approved. Then make the other poses by **editing the idle image**, changing only the arms, hands and face, so the body, legs, feet, hair and clothing stay pixel-identical wherever possible. Make `wave-2` by editing `wave-1`, changing only the raised hand and forearm. If a pose has to be regenerated from scratch, rescale and reposition it to match idle's registration (below) before delivering.

**Viewpoint:** a three-quarter front view, body turned slightly toward the **viewer's right**, as on the reference sheets. Make all 6 poses for both children face the same way. The game mirrors a character when it stands on the other side.

**What they wear and hold:** the standard outfit from the sheets. **Hands empty**: no backpack, no ears headband, no plush, no card, no props. Becca's ears and unicorn are items she packs in the game.

### Character technical specs
| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Format | PNG, **RGBA with a real transparent background**, 8-bit, sRGB |
| Canvas | **1024 x 1536 px (portrait), identical for every pose** |
| Baseline | The soles of the shoes rest exactly on **y = 1480 px** (56 px above the bottom edge) in every pose |
| Centering | The body's center line (between the feet, through the torso) is at **x = 512** in every pose |
| Jack-Jack's height | Soles to the top of the hair (standing poses, not counting raised hands): **1150 px** |
| Becca's height | Soles to the top of the hair: **1010 px** (she's 4, so about 88% of Jack-Jack). Count her curly hair as part of her. |
| Margins | Raised hands in `cheer` and `wave` must stay inside the canvas with at least 24 px clear at the top. Nothing touches any edge. |
| Scale | The same scale in every pose of a child. A head is the same size in `idle` and `cheer`. |
| Edges | A clean anti-aliased silhouette with the dark outline intact. **No white or colored halo, no leftover background pixels, no floor, no cast shadow** (the game adds a soft shadow). Check each cut-out over both black and white. |
| File size | under 2 MB each |

If your image tool can't output true transparency, generate the character on a perfectly flat, uniform **#00FFFF (cyan)** background that appears nowhere on the character, then remove it to transparency yourself. Deliver only the transparent PNGs.

**Verification to include:** for each child, a contact sheet of all 6 poses on a checkerboard, overlaid on one another at 30% opacity, which shows whether the feet and body line up. Also a JSON record per file with canvas size, the bounding box of non-transparent pixels, the lowest opaque y (should be 1480 +/- 2) and the measured standing height.

Suggested location: `Game Assets/Characters/Jack-Jack/` and `Game Assets/Characters/Becca/`, with the pose name in each filename (for example `Jack-Jack - cheer v0.1 - PROVISIONAL.png`).

## Request 3 - Props

All props are **transparent RGBA PNGs**, in the same cartoon style and lighting as the rooms, seen from the same slightly-above front angle as the room (like the suitcase in keyframe 06B). There's no background, floor or cast shadow, and the edges are clean.

### 3a. Suitcases - one set per child
Jack-Jack's is the **blue hard-shell** suitcase from the keyframes. Becca's is the **same model in pink**. For each child, three images on an **identical 1536 x 1024 canvas, with the suitcase in exactly the same position and size in all three**:

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `suitcase-open-back` | The open suitcase with its lid standing up behind and the **interior empty**. The front rim of the base is included. |
| `suitcase-open-front` | **Only the front rim/wall of the base** from the image above, everything else transparent. The game draws packed items between the back and front layers so they look tucked in. |
| `suitcase-closed` | The same suitcase closed and zipped, standing on its wheels, in the same footprint. |

The suitcase should fill about 85% of the canvas width.

### 3b. Clothing items - one set per child, with color variants
Match each child's **picture checklist card** (`Production Docs/Video Card - Jack v0.2.png` and `Video Card - Rebecca v0.2.png`, row 1) so the "correct" items in the game look exactly like the pictures on their card.

The game includes a matching game: the child finds their card's item among other versions of it (e.g., their red shirt among yellow, blue and green shirts). So each item needs the **card version plus 3 color variants**. Make each variant by **recoloring the card version** (same shape, same folds, only the main fabric color changes), so the only difference is color.

| Item | Files (per child) |
| --- | --- |
| `shirt` | `shirt-card` (exactly as on the card) + `shirt-alt-1`, `shirt-alt-2`, `shirt-alt-3` |
| `shorts` | `shorts-card` + 3 alts |
| `underwear` | `underwear-card` + 3 alts |
| `socks` | `socks-card` + 3 alts |
| `bundle` | The rolled "burrito" of the four card items rolled together, showing their colors in the spiral |

Variant colors: **alt-1 must be strongly different** from the card color (e.g., red vs. blue), because Becca's easy version only shows the card item plus alt-1. alt-2 and alt-3 can be closer to it (e.g., red vs. orange). Avoid colors that are hard to tell apart for color-blind players: don't use red vs. green as the only difference.

Views: folded flat-lay seen from above, the same view for all versions of an item. Specs: a **512 x 512** canvas with the item centered and filling about 80%; all versions of an item have identical size and position. Transparent RGBA, under 500 KB each. That's 17 files per child.

### 3c. Open dresser drawer
One **open drawer** that matches the honey-colored wooden dresser in both rooms. It's pulled out toward the viewer, the front face has the same round knobs, and the inside is visible and **empty**. The game lays it over the closed drawer and places items inside. Canvas **1024 x 768**, transparent, with the drawer filling about 90% of the width.

### 3d. Drawer close-up background
A full-screen **close-up looking down into the open dresser drawer** (the same honey-colored wood), with the interior empty and filling most of the frame. The game lays the clothing items out inside it and shows the target picture along the top. **Keep the top 25% of the image calm** (the drawer's back wall or the dresser top), because the target picture sits there. One image shared by both children. Same specs as the room backgrounds (16:9, 1920 x 1080 or larger, PNG, no text).

### 3e. Zipper pull
The suitcase zipper's pull tab as its own small sprite, which the player drags along the zipper. **256 x 256**, transparent. Make one per child, matching each suitcase's trim, and note the zipper's start and end points on the `suitcase-closed` image (pixel coordinates) in the verification record.

### 3f. Buttons
Two big round friendly buttons, **384 x 384**, transparent: **replay** (circular arrow) and **home** (simple house). No text.

### 3g. Check mark
One big friendly **green check mark** with a dark outline, in the style of the card checkboxes, to stamp onto a card checkbox. **512 x 512**, transparent.
